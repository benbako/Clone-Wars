Our position

Since 2009

A room should breathe. A building should belong. A home should remember what it is to be alive.

Sylvan Synergy works at the meeting point of architecture, landscape and the human nervous system. We design houses, cultural rooms and working gardens that lower the shoulders and sharpen the senses.

Our method is slow on purpose. We study the light before we draw a wall, choose materials that age in public, and plant for the fourth year rather than the first photograph.

The practice

Light · Material · Growth

Space is not a container. It is a conversation.

We design the conditions for a quieter kind of attention: the pause at a threshold, the cool of a stone floor, the moment a room fills with late light.

Biophilic design is often reduced to plants and timber. For us it is a structural idea. A building should behave like a living system — collecting light, moving air, holding water, making room for change.
