Questions

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Our studios in Lisbon, Kyoto and Vancouver support projects across Europe, Asia and North America, and we travel for site work.

What size of project do you take on?

From a single room to a small public building. We are most useful when we are involved early, before the site is fixed.

Can you work with our existing architect?

Often. We are happy to join as design partners for landscape, interiors or material strategy.

How do fees work?

We propose a fixed fee for each phase after the first conversation, so there are no surprises as the work develops.
